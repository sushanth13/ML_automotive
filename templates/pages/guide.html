Best use case

Prioritize

Treat the score as a prioritization aid for lead routing and follow-up urgency.

1. Enter the lead details

Choose a vehicle segment, shopper intent band, powertrain type, engagement recency, and intent score.

2. Run the prediction

Click Analyze Lead to send the form payload to the FastAPI prediction endpoint.

3. Read probability and badge

The probability shows the model confidence, while the badge gives a quick high or low likelihood summary.

4. Use analytics pages

Open the separate analytics and insight pages whenever you need more context behind the score.

Output interpretation

High likelihood Good candidate for rapid follow-up, richer offers, and stronger human attention.
Prioritize
Low likelihood Better suited to lower-cost nurture sequences until intent and recency improve.
Nurture
Practical reminder

A model score is strongest when paired with business context. Dealer availability, campaign goals, inventory realities, and timing should still shape the final outreach decision.
