Core finding

45%

EV and hybrid-oriented shoppers move through the funnel faster when intent and engagement signals both trend high.

Engagement recency wins

Leads touched within roughly five days retain much stronger conversion potential. Recency should remain one of the first fields reviewed before allocating premium sales effort.

Intent score above 80

High-intent shoppers are better candidates for direct follow-up, higher-touch CRM routing, and offer sequencing that assumes near-term readiness.

Creative matching matters

Regional and multicultural creative alignment can improve response quality, especially in large metro markets where audience fit affects campaign efficiency.

Protect outreach budgets

Use the classifier as a prioritization tool. Better ranking quality means expensive outreach can stay focused on leads most likely to respond.

Recommended operating rhythm

1. Score daily

Run fresh leads through the dashboard as soon as campaign and CRM events are updated.

2. Escalate high intent

Route high-scoring leads into fast-response workflows instead of pooled nurture queues.

3. Watch stale leads

Increase urgency once recency drifts, or accept that predicted conversion probability will soften.

4. Re-check monthly

Use the analytics pages to review whether model quality or market pressure assumptions changed.

Business takeaway

This system works best when teams use it as a ranking and prioritization layer, not as a fully autonomous decision engine. Pair the model score with campaign context and dealer follow-up capacity.
